Liberia Ministry

Our ministry serves communities throughout Liberia with a dual focus on spiritual growth and education. We are committed to planting vibrant, gospel-centered churches that meet the spiritual needs of Liberian families and strengthen local communities. Alongside our church planting efforts, we operate schools that provide quality education to children who might otherwise lack access to learning opportunities. Our educational programs combine academic excellence with Christian values, preparing students for both vocational success and meaningful citizenship. Through these integrated efforts, we aim to bring hope, transformation, and sustainable development to the people of Liberia. We believe that strong churches and strong schools work together to build stronger communities. Our team is dedicated to serving with compassion, integrity, and a long-term commitment to the Liberian people.
